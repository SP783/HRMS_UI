Compensatory Off — Expiry Policy

When an auto-earned comp-off expires, based on which week of the month it was earned in.

{% csrf_token %}

Displays a company-wide attendance status list (on leave, not checked in, half day, present) below Today's Birthdays. Visible to all staff. Scrolls when more than 5 people.

{% csrf_token %}

When off, every comp-off uses the single rule below regardless of which week it was earned.

Earned in this week or earlier = "within"; later = "after".

Default: comp-offs earned in the first 3 weeks must be used by the end of the same month; those earned in week 4+ are valid until the end of the next month.
